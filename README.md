# NearHelp

**Your Trusted Partner for Real-Time Local Assistance.**

NearHelp is a hyperlocal platform designed to connect people who need help with verified, reliable helpers in their neighborhood. whether you need assistance with daily chores, emergency tasks, or professional services, NearHelp bridges the gap instantly.

## 🌟 Why NearHelp?

We believe in building a community of trust and mutual assistance.
*   **Real-Time Connection:** Get help exactly when you need it.
*   **Verified Helpers:** Every helper completes a strict verification process including ID and selfie checks.
*   **Cash-Based:** Simple, transparent cash payments upon job completion.
*   **Local Focus:** Designed for Indian neighborhoods, available in your local language.

---

## 🚀 For Users
**Need a hand? We've got you covered.**

1.  **Post a Request:** Describe what you need, set the location, and offer a price.
2.  **Get Matched:** Nearby verified helpers are notified instantly.
3.  **Track & Confirm:** See when your helper arrives and track the job status.
4.  **Pay & Rate:** Pay cash once the job is done and rate your experience.

---

## 🤝 For Helpers
**Turn your spare time into earnings.**

1.  **Get Verified:** Upload your ID and selfie to become a trusted helper.
2.  **Find Jobs:** See requests near you and accept the ones that fit your schedule.
3.  **Earn Cash:** Get paid directly in cash immediately after completing the task.
4.  **Build Reputation:** High ratings unlock more opportunities.

---

## 🛡️ Trust & Safety First

Safety is our priority.
*   **Verification:** All helpers are verified by area supervisors.
*   **Moderation:** Active supervisors ensure fair play and resolve disputes.
*   **Community:** We block users or helpers who violate our community standards.
